01 / WHAT IS CAMPUSCODE?

WHERE STUDENTS BUILD.

CampusCode is an AI-powered hackathon platform — one place to run and join hackathons, instead of juggling many tools.

The problem today: a college hackathon runs on scattered tools — a form for registration, WhatsApp for updates, spreadsheets for scores, certificates made manually. Judging is slow and opaque, and participants have no single place to track everything.

For students: Discover events, register, join hackathons, find teammates with HackMate, submit ideas and projects, track rounds and results, and get verifiable certificates and a Digital ID card with QR.
For organisers: Create hackathons, manage registrations and approvals, run rounds, monitor submissions, and judge with AI help.
